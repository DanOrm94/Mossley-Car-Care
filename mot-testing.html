MOT Testing

DVSA-approved MOT testing to keep your vehicle safe, roadworthy, and legally compliant, every year.

Why It Matters

The MOT Test

An MOT test is a mandatory annual check that ensures vehicles meet the minimum safety and environmental standards required by UK law. Its purpose is to make sure cars on the road are safe to drive, properly maintained, and not producing excessive emissions that could harm the environment.

While an MOT does not assess the overall mechanical condition or longevity of a vehicle, it provides motorists with essential reassurance that key safety-critical components are functioning as they should.

A valid MOT certificate is a legal requirement for most vehicles over three years old, and driving without one can result in fines, penalty points, and potentially invalidated insurance.

What Gets Checked

Inside The Test

During an MOT, a qualified tester inspects a wide range of components across the vehicle to confirm they meet the required safety and environmental standards.

💡

Lights & Electrics

Lights, indicators, and electrics are checked to ensure they work correctly.

🚗

Brakes

Tested for performance and balance across the vehicle.

⚫

Tyres & Wheels

Tread depth and overall condition are assessed for safety.

🔧

Steering & Suspension

Examined for wear or excessive movement that could affect handling.

🛡️

Bodywork, Seatbelts & Windscreen

Checked for safety concerns such as damage, corrosion, or poor visibility.

💨

Exhaust & Emissions

The exhaust system is checked for leaks, and emissions are measured against required limits.

⛽

Fuel System

Checked for leaks and secure, safe operation.

🔭

Mirrors

Confirmed present, secure, and providing adequate visibility.

📋

Plates & Horn

Registration plates and the horn are checked for compliance and function.

In Summary

Safety, Roadworthiness & Compliance

The MOT test gives a thorough assessment of the safety, roadworthiness, and environmental compliance of your vehicle. While it doesn't replace regular servicing or maintenance, it ensures key systems are functioning correctly and helps keep both you and other road users safe.
