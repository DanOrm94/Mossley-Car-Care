What We Do

Our Services

Every service is carried out by qualified technicians using manufacturer-approved parts and modern diagnostic equipment, for every make and model.

01

MOT Testing

DVSA-approved MOT testing station. We test cars, vans and light commercials, with honest advice on any advisories.
02

Full & Interim Servicing

Manufacturer-schedule servicing to protect your warranty, including oil changes, filters, and multi-point checks.
03

Diagnostics

Computerised fault-finding for engine management lights, sensor faults, and electrical gremlins.
04

Brakes & Suspension

Pads, discs, shocks, and full suspension geometry checks to keep your car safe and stable.
05

Tyres & Alignment

Supply and fitting of a wide range of tyre brands, plus wheel alignment and balancing.
06

Air Conditioning

Re-gas, leak detection, and full air-con servicing to keep your cabin comfortable year-round.
07

Clutch & Gearbox

Clutch replacement and gearbox diagnostics for manual and automatic vehicles.

08

Batteries & Electrics

Battery testing and replacement, alternator checks, and general electrical fault repair.
09

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Considering a used car? We'll give it a thorough independent inspection before you buy.
