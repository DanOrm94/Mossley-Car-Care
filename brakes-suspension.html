Brakes & Suspension

Two of the most critical safety systems in your vehicle, kept in top condition to protect you and everyone else on the road.

Ride, Stability & Control

Suspension

The suspension system is one of the most important components of a car, responsible for ensuring a smooth, stable, and safe driving experience. It connects the vehicle's body to the wheels and includes parts such as springs, shock absorbers, struts, control arms, and linkages. Together, these components manage the impact of road irregularities and keep the tyres firmly in contact with the road.

A properly functioning suspension system improves comfort by absorbing bumps, potholes, and uneven surfaces. Without suspension, every imperfection on the road would be directly felt inside the car. More importantly, suspension plays a major role in maintaining vehicle stability and control.

Why It Matters

Why Suspension Is So Important

🛡️

Vehicle Safety & Control

Keeps all four tyres in contact with the road, which is essential for braking, steering, and cornering. Worn components increase the risk of accidents.

🚘

Improved Ride Comfort

Absorbs shocks from the road, reducing vibrations and jolts, especially on long trips or rough roads.

⚖️

Better Handling & Stability

Keeps the car balanced during turns, acceleration, and braking, reducing body roll and nose-diving.

⚫

Even Tyre Wear

Damaged or worn suspension parts cause uneven tyre wear, shortening tyre life and reducing grip.

🔧

Protects Other Components

Shields the vehicle's frame, steering, and braking components from damage caused by road shocks.

⚠️

Signs To Watch For

Excessive bouncing, pulling to one side, uneven tyre wear, nose-diving under braking, a rough ride, or visible fluid leaks from shock absorbers.

Stopping Safely

Brakes

Car brakes are one of the most crucial safety systems in any vehicle, and keeping them in top condition is essential for reliable, controlled driving. Effective brakes allow drivers to stop quickly in emergencies, maintain safe following distances, and navigate everyday traffic with confidence.

Over time, brake components such as pads, discs, and fluid naturally wear down, reducing performance and increasing stopping distances. Regular inspections and maintenance not only help prevent costly repairs but also ensure your vehicle remains safe for you, your passengers, and everyone else on the road.

Save Money, Stay Safe

Warning Signs & Early Action

Brake pad wear indicators alert you when your pads are nearing the end of their lifespan, appearing as a dashboard warning light or a high-pitched squealing sound.

🔈

Squealing Sound

A high-pitched squeal is often the first sign your pads need attention.

⚠️

Dashboard Warning Light

Brake wear indicators can also trigger a light on the dashboard.

⏳

Act Quickly

Acting quickly when these signs appear helps prevent the metal pad backing from grinding against the brake discs.

💀

Ignoring The Signs

Ignoring wear indicators can cause deep scoring, overheating, and require costly disc replacements.

✅

Reliable Braking

Responding promptly keeps braking performance safe and reliable.

💰

Save Money

Early action can save money by avoiding unnecessary repairs and extending the lifespan of the braking system.

📋

Included In Every Service

A full check of the braking system is included in a Service & Inspection, giving you confidence that this critical system is always looked after.
