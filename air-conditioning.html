Why It Matters

Air Con Repairs, Servicing & Re-Gas

Your vehicle's air conditioning system plays an important role in comfort, visibility, and overall driving experience. A properly working air con system helps keep the cabin cool in summer and can also assist with demisting windows during colder months.

We offer a range of air conditioning services, including system checks, re-gassing, leak detection, repairs, and component replacement where required.

Look Out For

Signs Your Car Air Con Needs Attention

Your air conditioning may need servicing if you notice any of the following.

Warm Air

Warm air instead of cold air coming from the vents.

Weak Airflow

Reduced or weak airflow from the vents.

Unpleasant Smells

Unpleasant odours when the system is switched on.

Noisy Operation

Unusual noises when the air con is running.

Slow Demisting

Windows taking longer than usual to demist.

Overdue Servicing

The air con has not been serviced for a long time.

Visible Leaks

Visible leaks or reduced cooling performance.
