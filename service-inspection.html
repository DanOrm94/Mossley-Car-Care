Why It Matters

Service & Inspection

Regular servicing and inspections are essential for keeping your car running safely, efficiently, and reliably. Over time, components such as oil, filters, brakes, tyres, and suspension parts naturally wear down, and without routine checks they can begin to affect performance or even lead to breakdowns.

A scheduled service ensures these critical items are inspected, cleaned, topped up, or replaced as needed, helping your vehicle operate smoothly and preventing small issues from developing into costly repairs.

Beyond reliability, regular maintenance also improves fuel efficiency, reduces emissions, and extends the overall lifespan of your car. Many manufacturers require servicing to maintain warranty cover, and a well-documented service history can boost resale value. By having your vehicle professionally serviced at recommended intervals, you gain peace of mind knowing it's safe, roadworthy, and performing at its best every day.

Look, Listen, Feel

Warning Signs

Motorists can often spot early signs that their car may need a service or inspection simply by paying attention to how it looks, sounds, and feels.

🔊

Unusual Noises

Knocking, squealing, or grinding sounds can point to issues with brakes, belts, or engine components.

⚠️

Dashboard Warning Lights

Engine, oil, or battery indicators are clear signals that something needs attention.

⚙️

Performance Changes

Reduced engine performance, poor fuel economy, rough idling, or difficulty starting.

💧

Visible Fluid Leaks

Oil leaks, excessive exhaust smoke, or low fluid levels suggest a professional check is due.

🚗

Uneven Tyre Wear

Irregular wear patterns can indicate alignment, suspension, or pressure issues.

📅

Time For A Check

Typically every 12 months or 10,000–12,000 miles, depending on the manufacturer.

Don't Wait For Symptoms

Prevention Is Better Than Cure

While warning signs are helpful, relying on them alone isn't enough. Regular, periodic servicing plays a vital role in preventing problems before they arise. Routine inspections keep essential components in good condition, ensure the vehicle remains safe and efficient, and maintain long-term reliability.
