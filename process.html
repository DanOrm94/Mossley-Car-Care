How It Works

Our Process

Transparent, straightforward, and built on trust. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you book to the moment you drive away.

01

Book Online or by Phone

Choose a date and time that suits you through our booking page, or give us a call. We'll confirm your slot the same day.

02

Drop Off Your Vehicle

Bring your car in at the agreed time. We offer a comfortable waiting area, or you're welcome to leave your keys with us for the day.

03

Full Inspection

Our qualified technicians carry out the MOT or service to the letter, checking every item on the statutory list plus a full multi-point check.

04

Honest Report

We'll call or message you with a clear, jargon-free explanation of any advisories or required work, with no pressure to say yes.

05

Approve Any Extra Work

You decide what work goes ahead. We provide a written quote before touching anything beyond the original booking.

06

Collect & Drive Away

Pay securely on collection, receive your MOT certificate or service record, and get back on the road with confidence.
