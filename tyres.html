Why It Matters

Tyres

Tyres play a crucial role in your vehicle's safety, performance, and fuel efficiency, as they are the only point of contact between your car and the road. Worn or damaged tyres can significantly reduce grip — especially in wet conditions — and increase stopping distances, making regular checks essential.

UK law requires tyres to have a minimum tread depth of 1.6mm across the central three-quarters of the tread and to be free from cuts, bulges, or other defects that could compromise safety.

We offer a full tyre service, including inspections, advice on suitable replacements, and a wide range of quality tyres to suit all budgets. We can supply and professionally fit new tyres on-site, ensuring your vehicle remains safe, compliant, and ready for the road.

Quick & Reliable

How To Check Your Tyre Tread

Inbuilt tyre wear indicators, also known as Tread Wear Indicators (TWIs), are small, raised bars moulded into the grooves of your tyre's tread.

🔬

What Are TWIs?

Small, raised bars moulded into the tread grooves. They sit slightly lower than the tread when the tyre is new.

🔄

As Tyres Wear

As the rubber wears down, these bars become more visible against the surrounding tread.

⚠️

Level With The Tread

When the tread surface becomes level with the indicators, the tyre has reached the legal minimum tread depth of 1.6mm and should be replaced immediately.

✅

No Tools Needed

TWIs provide a quick, reliable way to check tyre wear without needing any tools.

🛡️
